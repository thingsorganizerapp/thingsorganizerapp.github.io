# Privacy Policy for Things Organizer

**Last updated:** November 24, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Things Organizer ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application (the "Service").

**Information We Collect**

Things Organizer is a local-first application. All data you create and use within the app is stored solely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information to our servers or any third-party services.

The data you create, which is stored locally, includes:

*   **Things:** The items you create, including their names and descriptions.
*   **Thing Tags:** The tags you create and associate with your Things.

**How We Use Your Information**

Your data is used only to provide you with the functionality of the Things Organizer app. Because we do not collect your data, we do not use it for any other purpose.

**Data Sharing**

We do not share your data with any third parties because we do not have access to it.

**Data Security**

We are committed to ensuring that your information is secure. All data for Things Organizer is stored in a local database on your device and is protected by the security features of your mobile operating system.

**Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

**Contact Us**

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
